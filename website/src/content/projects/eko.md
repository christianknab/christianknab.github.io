# Eko Social Media App
Back in sophomore year, a couple friends and I decided to build a social media app that would mimic how social media used to be. Remember when your feed was just chronological posts from the friends you followed? Eko would have no algorithms, no "suggested" crap to keep you hooked on the app, just a feed of the people you actually care about.

As we built it, Eko morphed into a place where our friends and other people at UCSC could post text and GIFs and create memes. After beta testing it and working out the major bugs, we released it and people started downloading and posting. 

Surprisingly, the app really started to get popular. People were posting a lot, requesting more features, and interacting with each other. While at the beginning most of the people were from UCSC, there were interactions between different "groups" that never would have happened. That I think is the most surprising part of the app.

Over time, we started adding more features like Groups, "ekos" (re-tweets), and Polls. One of my favorite features we added was ascii art images. We wanted to add image sharing, but didn't want to pay a monthly Google Cloud bill to store them. So we decided to build an [images to ascii](https://pub.dev/packages/image_to_ascii) package and come up with our own little compression format to save as much space as possible. Now, its one of Eko's fun gimmicks.

To this day, we keep updating the app and have some cool plans for the future. We're currently working on [Eko Messenger](https://github.com/eko-network/eko-messenger)) ato bring federated E2EE messaging to the platform, and refining the social part of the app to make communties more of a focus.

If you want to check it out, give it a download!
